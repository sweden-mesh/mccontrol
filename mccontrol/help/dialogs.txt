Questions

A verb that needs values asks for them -- the one or two it needs, such
as a region, a command, a name -- and sends nothing until you answer.
Backing out of the question sends nothing. Earlier commands can be
recalled where a command is typed.

A question about a command that takes something away from a repeater
says so, and answering it is the only way that command is sent. See
[[cautions]].
