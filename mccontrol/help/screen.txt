The screen

The left panel is the device directory: one row per repeater you manage,
with its name, node id, region, when you last sent to it, its battery and
last-seen age once asked for, and what it answered. The row under the cursor bar is the current device; Ins or Space
tags a row, * inverts the tags, + and - tag or untag a whole region. The
panel's bottom edge counts the tags.

The right panel shows what the cursor is on: the device's history, newest
first. After a send it shows that send's results per device until the
cursor moves; after a discovery it shows what answered. Tab moves between
the panels; Enter on a history line opens the whole reply.

The top line is the menu bar (F10 or Alt+letter). The bottom line is the
key bar: the ten function keys and what each does here. The line above it
is the message line: the outcome of the last thing you did.

See [[keys]] for the keys, [[send]] for sending, [[discover]] for finding
repeaters.

Batt and Seen come from Beacon when you ask (r); see [[stats]].

A name ending in " ?" marks a device something was sent to and nothing
heard from for seven days -- no result over the control plane and no
status seen by Beacon. It is a device to look at, not a verdict: the
control plane may be off, or the node may answer another key now. A
device never sent to carries no mark, since nothing is known. Nothing
refreshes by itself; s, r and n ask when you do.

Every wait -- a send, a discovery, a set, a read from Beacon -- is a box
over the work with what is happening, the running count and a bar over
the wait. Esc stops waiting early and keeps what has come.

The right side is two panels: the history (or a send's results) above,
and "Last answers" below -- what the cursor device last said to each read,
with its age (see [[reads]]).
