The screen

The left panel is the device directory: one row per repeater you manage,
with its name, node id, region, when you last sent to it and what it
answered. The row under the cursor bar is the current device; Ins or Space
tags a row, * inverts the tags, + and - tag or untag a whole region. The
panel's bottom edge counts the tags.

The right panel shows what the cursor is on: the device's history, newest
first. After a send it shows that send's results per device until the
cursor moves; after a discovery it shows what answered. Tab moves between
the panels; Enter on a history line opens the whole reply.

The top line is the menu bar (F10 or Alt+letter). The bottom line is the
key bar: the ten function keys and what each does here. The line above it
is the message line: the outcome of the last thing you did.

See [[keys]] for the keys, [[send]] for sending, [[discover]] for finding
repeaters.
