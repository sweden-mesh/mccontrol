Help: contents

mccontrol signs commands with your key and sends them to the repeaters you
run, over the control broker. Tab moves to the next link, Enter follows
it, F12 goes back, F11 shows the contents, F3 leaves help.

Getting around
  [[screen]]      the main menu and Work with Devices
  [[keys]]        every function key, option and command
  [[dialogs]]     prompts, Confirm screens, waits

Doing things
  [[commandlist]] 7=Commands: every command, with its parameter asked for
  [[send]]        send a command to one device, the tagged ones, or a region
  [[shortcuts]]   Shift+F1-F9: a command per key, no prompt
  [[sets]]        command sets: a rollout as one named list of steps
  [[discover]]    find the repeaters that accept your key
  [[devices]]     add, change, forget; names and regions
  [[history]]     what was sent and what came back
  [[reads]]       8=Last answers: what the device last answered to each read
  [[stats]]       6=Statistics: battery, airtime, noise floor, from Beacon

Reference
  [[commands]]    the commands a repeater admits over the control plane
  [[cautions]]    the commands that take something away
  [[signing]]     your key, provisioning a repeater, what the signature covers
  [[assurance]]   can a command be forged, altered or replayed? what is not covered
  [[broker]]      the control broker, its credentials, the connection test
  [[protocol]]    the envelope on the wire, for the curious
  [[cli]]         the same work from the command line
