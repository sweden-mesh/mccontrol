The screen

The main menu is a numbered list: type the number on the ===> line and
press Enter (see [[keys]] for every selection). The box at the right says
which broker you use, whether there is a signing key and whether it is
registered, and how many devices you have.

Work with Devices (1) is the device directory: one row per repeater you
manage, with its name, node id, region, when you last sent to it, its
battery and last-seen age once asked for, and what it answered. A ">"
before a row marks it tagged: type 1 in its Opt column and press Enter.
"tag <region>", "untag" and "invert" on the ===> line tag a region,
clear the tags, invert them. The count of tags and the sort are at the
right above the headings.

Type an option by any number of rows and press Enter; each runs in turn.
5=Display shows the device and its history (see [[history]]), 8=Facts
what is known about it: its settings, firmware and regions, each with its
age, "unsigned" where older firmware answered (see [[reads]]).

The line above the function keys is the message line: the outcome of the
last thing you did, or why it was refused.

Batt and Seen come from Beacon when you ask (F7); see [[stats]].

A name ending in " ?" marks a device something was sent to and nothing
heard from for seven days -- no result over the control plane and no
status seen by Beacon. It is a device to look at, not a verdict: the
control plane may be off, or the node may answer another key now. A
device never sent to carries no mark, since nothing is known. Nothing
refreshes by itself; 6, F7 and 11 ask when you do.

Every wait -- a send, a discovery, a set, a read from Beacon -- shows its
progress and the seconds waited on the message line; F12 stops waiting
early and keeps what has come.

On an 80-column terminal not every column fits: 11 on the main menu
picks which of Reg, Sent, Batt, Seen, Ver and Result show after the name
and node ("mccontrol config --columns ver,result" does the same).

See [[send]] for sending, [[discover]] for finding repeaters.
