Overlays

A verb that needs values draws an overlay over the board.

A question with one field: type, Enter answers; where a command is typed,
Up and Down recall earlier ones. A form with several fields (edit, the
broker, a command set, the shortcuts): Tab or Down to the next field,
Shift+Tab or Up to the one before, Enter on the last field or F2
anywhere saves. A list to pick from: type to filter, Up and Down move,
Enter picks; where several may be chosen, Space toggles and Enter takes
them. Esc closes any of them and sends nothing.

The broker form's tls field takes on or off: TLS on 8883 is the command
plane; plain 1883 takes no commands.

A command that takes something away from a repeater asks in an overlay
of its own, in the alert colour: "y" goes ahead, any other key sends
nothing. See [[cautions]].
