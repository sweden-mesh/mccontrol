Devices

A device is a repeater you manage: a name you choose, its node id (the
first eight hex characters of its public key, which is how the firmware
names it on the control topics), the region its observers use (the IATA
code, such as JKG), and notes.

"e" edits the cursor device: name, region, notes. Del forgets it after a
"y"; its history file stays on disk. Devices are added by [[discover]]
or by hand on the command line (see [[cli]]).

A device with no region cannot be sent to; give it one with "e". A
region belongs to the device, not to the program: you may run repeaters
in several.

Names and versions from the fleet. "n" sends "get name" and "ver" to the
tagged devices, or the cursor one: a device you have not named takes the
name the node answers with, a name you gave is kept, and every device
stores its firmware version, shown short in the Ver column (1.17.1-ar-10)
and sortable ("o"). Two signed reads per device, each one of the node's
command slots this hour; more than three devices asks first. Firmware
that admits neither read answers not-allowed and the device keeps what
it had. "mccontrol refresh" does the same from the command line.

The whole key. A node on the firmware of 2026-10-08 or later (ar-11)
listens on a topic named by its whole 64-character key; older firmware
by the first 8. The tool keeps the whole key on the device when it
knows it -- a discovery sees it on the node's answer, and "mccontrol add
<name> <64 hex> <region>" stores it -- and addresses each node by the
form its firmware uses. The 8-character id stays the name you see.
