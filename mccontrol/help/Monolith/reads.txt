Stats: what is known about a repeater

The STATS tile follows the cursor and shows what the device has told you,
parsed into settings, each with the age of the answer that carried it:

  name, firmware            "get name", "ver"
  tx delay, rx delay,       "get txdelay", "get rxdelay",
  direct tx delay           "get direct.txdelay"
  autoreply, its hops,      "get autoreply" (one answer, six facts), or
  mode, channel, ...        each "get autoreply.<setting>"
  autoreply slots, slot,    on firmware that has them; "autoreply delay"
  window                    on older firmware
  regions                   "region": the region map
  anything else             every "get <setting>" is a fact by its name

An answer whose signature failed (BAD SIGNATURE) teaches nothing. An
unsigned one -- from firmware older than ar-11, or kept before the tool
recorded signatures -- still counts, drawn dim and marked "unsigned"
where the tile has room ("dim: unsigned" at its foot says so where it has
not). A value too long for the tile ends in "…".

A write teaches as much as a read: "set txdelay 0.7" answered OK makes
the tx delay 0.7, as of then. The newest answer wins; a refusal or
silence teaches nothing and clears nothing, so an old value stays with
its age showing how old it is. A read the tile does not know how to parse
(neighbors, board, memory, the "stats (Beacon)" line) is listed after the
facts, raw, under its command.

Nothing is sent to show it: it comes from what is kept on this machine,
the last answer to each read and the history of every command, and it
changes the moment a result lands. Enter on a row reads it again (Tab to
the tile first); Up and Down move.

The same list is "GET /devices/{ref}/facts" on the API. See
[[commandlist]], [[history]] and [[stats]].
