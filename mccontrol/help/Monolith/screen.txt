The board

The board is a set of tiles; Tab moves the focus from one to the next,
and the focused tile's frame is drawn bright.

DEVICES is the device directory: one row per repeater you manage, with
its name, node id, region, when you last sent to it, its battery and
last-seen age once asked for, and what it answered. The row under the
cursor is the current device; Space or Ins tags it, "*" inverts the tags,
"+" and "-" tag or untag a whole region. The tile's bottom edge counts
the tags and says the sort.

STATS follows the cursor: the node, its region and version, its
statistics as sparklines once read, and what is known about it -- tx
and rx delay, autoreply and its settings, regions, firmware, name --
each with its age (see [[reads]]). Enter on a row asks it again.

RESULTS is a console: each send, set and discovery as it lands, oldest
at the top and the newest just above its command line, the running job
inline as a bar. A long result shows its start. Tab (or a click) on the
bottom line types a command there; Enter sends it, as "c" would (see
[[send]]). On the results themselves, Enter opens the whole result. A
short note at the bottom left says how the last thing came out.

STATUS shows the broker, your key, whether it is registered and whether
the broker test passed, and on a wide terminal how you sign in and the
jobs kept -- no taller than those lines. On a wide terminal a clock is
drawn above it.

HISTORY is what was sent and what came back, one block per device, newest
block first: the device's short id once on the left, then "> command" and
"< reply" for each of its last commands. A send to a whole region is a
block of its own ("JKG*"), its reply the count that answered. A reply
whose signature failed is drawn in the alert colour as "BAD SIGNATURE";
an unsigned one (older firmware) is dim; a cut line ends in "…". It is under
STATUS on a wide terminal and under DEVICES at 80x24; Tab to it and Up,
Down, PgUp and PgDn scroll it. "h" opens the cursor device's full history.

The bottom line is the hints: a key and its verb, each one clickable.

Batt and Seen come from Beacon when you ask ("r"); see [[stats]].

A name ending in " ?" marks a device something was sent to and nothing
heard from for seven days -- no result over the control plane and no
status seen by Beacon. It is a device to look at, not a verdict: the
control plane may be off, or the node may answer another key now. A
device never sent to carries no mark, since nothing is known. Nothing
refreshes by itself; "s", "r" and "n" ask when you do.

Every wait -- a send, a discovery, a set, a read from Beacon -- is a bar
in the results tile with the running count; Esc stops waiting early and
keeps what has come.

Not every column fits a narrow directory: "directory columns" in the
palette picks which of Reg, Sent, Batt, Seen, Ver and Result show after
the name and node ("mccontrol config --columns ver,result" does the
same).

See [[keys]] for the keys, [[send]] for sending, [[discover]] for finding
repeaters.
