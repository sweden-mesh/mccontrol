Sending a command

"c" asks for a command and sends it to the tagged devices, or to the
cursor device when nothing is tagged; the overlay says which. Up recalls
earlier commands; Enter sends. Each device gets its own signed publish;
each answer lands in the results tile as it comes back, and a note says
how many answered.

"a" sends to every node in a region instead: it asks for the region,
then the command, and makes one signed publish to the region's broadcast
topic, answered by every node there that holds your key. A command that
transmits on the air (a "trigger") cannot be sent to a region; the
firmware refuses it and so does this.

Tagged devices in two different regions cannot be sent to at once: a send
is to one region.

Commands that take something away ask first; see [[cautions]]. For what a
repeater admits, see [[commands]]. A refusal is explained in words:
"refused: not-allowed" means the firmware does not admit that command;
"rate-limited" means wait a few minutes; "sig-invalid" means the node
holds a different public key than yours (see [[signing]]).
