History

Every command sent to a device is kept with what came back: the time, the
command, the topic, the result code in words and the reply. A device's
history is shown newest first, and any line opens the whole reply.

Broadcasts are kept once per device asked and once under "all" with how
many answered.

The files are under your configuration directory, history/<node>.jsonl,
one line of JSON per command. See [[cli]] for "mccontrol history".
