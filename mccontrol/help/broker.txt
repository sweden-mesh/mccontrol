The control broker

Commands and results travel through one MQTT broker, the one each
repeater's mqtt.cmd.slot points at. Options > Broker sets its
host, port, username, password and how long to wait for answers. Options
> Test the broker connects with those, subscribes to a test topic,
publishes to it and checks the broker delivers it back; each step is
reported and the first failure named ("Not authorized" is the broker
rejecting the username or password).

This is not the broker the repeaters' statistics go to; those credentials
are not used here.
