The control broker

Commands and results travel through one MQTT broker, the one each
repeater's mqtt.cmd.slot points at. The configuration holds its host,
port, username, password and how long to wait for answers. The broker
test connects with those, subscribes to a test topic, publishes to it
and checks the broker delivers it back; each step is reported and the
first failure named ("Not authorized" is the broker rejecting the
username or password). "mccontrol config" and "mccontrol test" do the
same; where they are in your interface is in [[keys]].

This is not the broker the repeaters' statistics go to; those credentials
are not used here.

Back to [[index]].

Signing in. With no username set, the tool signs in to the broker the
way a node does: the username is "v1_" plus your public key and the
password a token signed by your key, renewed each session -- nothing to
hand out, nothing to type. That is how the default broker,
mqtt.louie.se, knows who is publishing. A region running its own broker
with a username and password sets them here, and then those are used.
