Sending a command

F2 opens the send dialog addressed to the tagged devices, or to the cursor
device when nothing is tagged. Type the command (Up recalls earlier ones)
and press Enter. Each tagged device gets its own signed publish; the
results appear in the right panel as they come back, one line per device,
and the message line says how many answered.

F7 sends to every node in a region instead: one signed publish to the
region's broadcast topic, answered by every node there that holds your
key. A command that transmits on the air (a "trigger") cannot be sent to
a region; the firmware refuses it and so does this.

Tagged devices in two different regions cannot be sent to at once: a send
is to one region.

Commands that take something away ask first; see [[cautions]]. For what a
repeater admits, see [[commands]]. A refusal is explained in words:
"refused: not-allowed" means the firmware does not admit that command;
"rate-limited" means wait a few minutes; "sig-invalid" means the node
holds a different public key than yours (see [[signing]]).
