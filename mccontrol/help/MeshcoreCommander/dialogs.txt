Dialogs

A dialog asks for the one or two values a verb needs. Type into the
highlighted field; Tab and Shift+Tab move between fields and buttons; Up
and Down in the command field recall earlier commands; Enter presses the
default button (the highlighted one) from any field; Esc cancels and
sends nothing.

A dialog that would take something away from a repeater says so in red
and offers [ Send anyway ] and [ Cancel ], with Cancel as the default.
See [[cautions]].
