Devices

A device is a repeater you manage: a name you choose, its node id (the
first eight hex characters of its public key, which is how the firmware
names it on the control topics), the region its observers use (the IATA
code, such as JKG), and notes.

F4 edits the cursor device. F8 forgets it after a confirmation; its
history file stays on disk. Devices are added by [[discover]] or by hand
on the command line (see [[cli]]).

A device with no region cannot be sent to; set one with F4. A region
belongs to the device, not to the program: you may run repeaters in
several.
