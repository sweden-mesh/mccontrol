Last answers: what a repeater has told you

The lower right panel keeps what the cursor device last answered to
each read -- ver, get name, region, neighbors, every get -- one line
each: the age of the answer, the command, the reply; newest first. It
follows the cursor, so moving onto a device shows at once what is known
about it without sending anything.

A write (set ...) is not kept there; history has it. A refusal or
silence stores nothing and clears nothing: the last good answer stays,
and its age says how old it is. The statistics view leaves a
"stats (Beacon)" line with the battery, uptime and noise floor.

Tab moves between the three panels; on the lower one, Enter sends that
read again and the line is replaced when the answer arrives. On the
command line, "mccontrol reads <device>" prints the same.

See [[commandlist]], [[history]] and [[stats]].
