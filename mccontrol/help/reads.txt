Last answers: what a repeater has told you

What the current device last answered to each read -- ver, get name,
region, neighbors, every get -- is kept, one line each: the age of the
answer, the command, the reply; newest first. It follows the current
device, so moving onto a device shows at once what is known about it
without sending anything.

A write (set ...) is not kept there; history has it. A refusal or
silence stores nothing and clears nothing: the last good answer stays,
and its age says how old it is. The statistics view leaves a
"stats (Beacon)" line with the battery, uptime and noise floor.

Any line can be read again, and is replaced when the answer arrives. On
the command line, "mccontrol reads <device>" prints the same.

See [[commandlist]], [[history]] and [[stats]].
