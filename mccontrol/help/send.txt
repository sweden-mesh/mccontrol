Sending a command

A send goes to the tagged devices, or to the current device when nothing
is tagged. Each device gets its own signed publish; the results are shown
as they come back, one line per device, with how many answered.

A send to every node in a region is one signed publish to the region's
broadcast topic, answered by every node there that holds your key. A
command that transmits on the air (a "trigger") cannot be sent to a
region; the firmware refuses it and so does this.

Tagged devices in two different regions cannot be sent to at once: a send
is to one region.

Commands that take something away ask first; see [[cautions]]. For what a
repeater admits, see [[commands]]. A refusal is explained in words:
"refused: not-allowed" means the firmware does not admit that command;
"rate-limited" means wait a few minutes; "sig-invalid" means the node
holds a different public key than yours (see [[signing]]).
